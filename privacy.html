What stays on your phone

Your name, date, time and place of birth, language choice and settings are stored only on your device. We have no user accounts. Photos you choose for FLAMES cards are used on your phone and are not uploaded. You can delete saved birth details inside the app (Kundali screen) or by uninstalling the app.

Location

If you allow location, it is used on your device to calculate Panchang times (sunrise, Rahu Kaal and similar). It is not sent to our servers.

AI chat

When you ask the AI astrologer a question, the question and a short summary of your birth chart are sent to our server and to an AI provider (Google Gemini or Cloudflare AI) to create a reply. We do not store the text of your questions. We keep a temporary hourly counter linked to your network address to limit misuse.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads, and may ask for your consent where the law requires. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

Rising Stars is not directed at children under 18.

Entertainment only

Horoscopes, kundali, compatibility, muhurat and AI replies are for entertainment and cultural interest. They are not medical, legal, financial or life advice.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
