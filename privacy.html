Astro Pandit ✨ Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Contact: astropanditji2000@gmail.com

What stays on your phone

Your name, date, time and place of birth, language choice and settings are stored only on your device. We have no user accounts. Photos you choose for FLAMES cards are used on your phone and are not uploaded. You can delete saved birth details inside the app (Kundali screen) or by uninstalling the app. Birth details you choose to send on WhatsApp go to our team — see Ask Panditji below.

Location and place search

If you allow location, the app asks only for your approximate (city-level) location. It is used on your device to calculate Panchang times (sunrise, Rahu Kaal and similar). To show your place name, your approximate coordinates are sent to a free reverse-geocoding service (BigDataCloud). When you search for a birth place, the text you type is sent to a free geocoding service (Open-Meteo). We do not receive or store either.

Ask Panditji

There is no AI in this app. When you use Ask Panditji, your question and birth details go to our pandit team on WhatsApp, and they reply to you there. We do not send your questions to any AI provider, and no reply is generated by AI. We do not promise a reply time.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and Google Firebase may collect your device advertising ID and similar device data to show and measure ads, and may ask for your consent where the law requires. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Notifications

Daily notifications are created on your phone. If you allow notifications, the app also receives announcements from us through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which gives your device an anonymous token. We do not link it to your name or birth details.

Contacting us

The Ask Panditji section opens WhatsApp with a message you can edit before sending. Anything you send is shared only by your choice, with the number shown in the app.

Children

Astro Pandit is not directed at children under 18.

Entertainment only

Horoscopes, kundali, compatibility, muhurat and personal readings are for entertainment and cultural interest. They are not medical, legal, financial or life advice.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
